Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

ProxyDisc has no accounts and no database of users. This page explains the small amount of data involved in using it.

Data stored in your browser

The discs you save, your loadouts, upgrade history, tracked agents and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data is not sent to ProxyDisc or anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes it; the Backup tab lets you export it first.

The contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, the name, optional email address and message you enter are sent through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, so the message can reach the maintainer by email. Your email address is only used to reply to you, and only if you choose to include it. Messages are not used for marketing or shared with anyone else. You can read the Web3Forms privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

ProxyDisc is free and supported by advertising served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For more on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are shown a consent choice before personalised ads are used.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may record standard technical information such as IP addresses in server logs for security and performance. ProxyDisc does not use this to identify visitors.

Children

ProxyDisc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact form in the tool.
